features:
  - title: Your model, your gateway
    details: Local models through Ollama, llama-server or LM Studio, or cloud models through a gateway you run that holds your key. smalt never sees or holds a key.
  - title: What you grant
    details: Schema only, statistics, sample rows or full data, per database, narrowed per table and column. Changes go through the app's own actions.
  - title: Everything logged, in your browser
    details: Every request, response and action is logged where you can read and export it. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
